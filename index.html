Relax & Explore Zanzibar⌂ Meliá Zanzibar›

Enjoy three full independent days at Meliá Zanzibar, with time to relax on the Indian Ocean and explore the island at your own pace.

Highlights

  • Beach and pool time at Meliá Zanzibar, with warm Indian Ocean waters and time simply to unwind.
  • Stone Town: explore Zanzibar’s UNESCO-listed historic center, winding lanes, markets, architecture, and waterfront.
  • Spice farm experience: discover the cloves, vanilla, cinnamon, nutmeg, and other spices that shaped Zanzibar’s history and culture.
  • Snorkeling or a marine excursion, including the waters around Mnemba Atoll when conditions and availability allow.
  • Traditional dhow sailing or a sunset cruise along the Zanzibar coast.
  • Jozani Forest: look for Zanzibar’s endemic red colobus monkeys and learn about the island’s unique ecosystem.
  • Prison Island / Changuu Island: combine history with a visit to the island’s giant Aldabra tortoises.
  • Cultural experiences such as a local village visit, cooking experience, market visit, or Swahili food tour.

Logistics

  • Your Meliá Zanzibar stay is independently arranged and is separate from your Prominent Africa safari services.
  • None of the activities listed above have been booked. We suggest contacting the Activities Concierge at Meliá Zanzibar to discuss options, availability, timing, and reservations.
  • We recommend asking Meliá Zanzibar to arrange both your airport arrival transfer on October 2 and your departure transfer to Zanzibar International Airport on October 6.
  • Please reconfirm your October 6 departure-transfer pickup time with the hotel during your stay, allowing ample time for international check-in and security.

FAQ
General Information
What should we expect from each safari day?

Your days remain flexible. Wildlife is often most active early and late, but Steve will adjust timing, meals, and breaks around your interests, energy, and current conditions.

What luggage works best?

Soft-sided bags are best for safari vehicles and bush flights. Auric Air currently lists a 20 kg baggage allowance per adult, inclusive of hand luggage.

Who is our guide?

Steve Nyiti is your Prominent Africa safari host and guide unless we advise you of a specific operational change.

Money Matters
What currency should we carry?

Bring newer U.S. dollar bills in good condition, preferably 2009 or later, along with some Tanzanian shillings for small local purchases.

Are cards accepted?

Major hotels and many camps accept cards, but cash remains useful for small purchases, local markets, and gratuities.

Safety & Health Matters
How do we stay safe around wildlife?

Remain inside the vehicle unless Steve confirms an area is safe, never feed wildlife, and follow camp escort rules after dark.

What about sun and hydration?

Use high-SPF sunscreen, wear a hat, and drink water regularly. The sun can be strong even when temperatures feel comfortable.

💵 Gratuities & Tipping

Gratuities are an important part of the hospitality and tourism industry throughout East Africa and are a meaningful way to recognize exceptional service. While tipping is always at your discretion, it is customary and greatly appreciated.

East Africa Safari

Our Recommendation

Private Safari Guide: $10–25 USD per person, per day
Lodge or Camp Staff: $20 USD per person, per day, shared tip box
Hotel Staff: $5 USD per person, per day
Private Airport or Hotel Transfer: $5 USD per vehicle

Most safari lodges and camps provide a shared staff tip box near reception. Your safari guide is usually tipped directly at the conclusion of your safari.

Additional Tipping Situations

Airport porter: $2 per bag. Hotel bell staff: $2 per bag. Housekeeping: $1–2 per room, per day. Restaurant server: 5–10% when no service charge is included. Taxi driver: round up or add $2–5 for exceptional help. Spa or local activity staff: $2–5.

Additional Guidance

Carry $1, $5, $10, and $20 bills. Cash is generally preferred. Check bills for included service charges. “Asante” means “thank you” in Swahili. When uncertain, ask us.

Above all, gratuities should reflect your personal experience. Exceptional service may inspire additional recognition, and every gratuity is a meaningful expression of appreciation.
